# 🪶 PROJECTGOMAD

Building worlds — some made of cubes, some on land, and some at sea.  

I’m **ProjectGoMad**, also known as *diskawrs* — a creator balancing between code, craft, and tide.

---

### 🧭 GoMad / GoLive

“GoLive” isn’t just about streaming.  
It’s about *living* — finding balance between digital creation and the tangible world.  
Some days I’m live on **Twitch**, scripting Denizen logic for the Minecraft RPG world *A Game of Cubes*.  
Other days, I’m live on the water aboard **Skellig**, a 1970s *Écume de Mer* undergoing an honest, rolling refit.  

Either way, it’s all part of the same story — creativity, community, and a bit of chaos.

---

### 🧩 Ongoing projects

- 🧱 **[A Game of Cubes](https://agameofcubes.com)** — community-driven RPG Minecraft world with Towny, Denizen, and MythicMobs.  
- ⚓ **[Skellig](https://projectgomad.com/index.php/skellig-ecume-de-mar/)** — 1976 *Écume de Mer* sailboat restoration and small-craft experiments.  
- 💬 **[Twitch](https://twitch.com/projectgomad)** — Twitch & social projects focused on creative balance, design, and storytelling.
